Journal

Viridis was founded with community in mind. As two women pursuing science at NIS PhM Almaty, we noticed a lack of visibility, accessibility, and engagement within the science community in Kazakhstan. We wanted to create a platform that removes barriers related to age, background, and experience, allowing passion to remain at the forefront.

Viridis Journal offers anyone with a zeal for science — including natural sciences, social sciences, and the humanities — the opportunity to publish their work, share experiences, and support their cause.

Follow us on Instagram at @viridis.society and engage with the articles our team has worked so hard to create.
